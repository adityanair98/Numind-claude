# Search-Demand Research — High-Intent Queries → Product Packaging (June 2026)

**Question:** Which high-impact, high-intent US search queries should shape the Block-1b lesson wave, the web/SEO surface, and the TikTok content engine?
**Method:** 5 parallel domain-cluster research agents (paycheck/taxes · credit/debt/BNPL · car/housing · benefits/job-change · scams/fees/everyday), sourced from keyword studies (Semrush/Ahrefs-published data), Google Year-in-Search, FTC/KFF/EBRI/Edmunds data, SERP-density analysis, and recurring Reddit threads. Volume figures are as-reported by sources (directional, not verified ad-platform numbers).
**Cross-referenced against:** the live catalog (118 lessons / 692 templates / 12 calculators / 121 programmatic web pages).

---

## 1. Headline findings

1. **The catalog already owns most of the head terms.** The biggest validated queries map to *existing* lessons: bonus-tax shock → `salary_bonus_after_tax`; "what is APR" (~78K/mo across two phrasings) → `debt_apr_vs_apy`; statement-close timing → `debt_cc_statement_cycle`; utilization myth → `concept_debt_credit_scores`; affordability trio (house/rent/car) → existing lessons + calculators; HDHP/FSA/coinsurance → insurance module; snowball-vs-avalanche, balance transfer, 50/30/20, emergency fund, Roth-vs-traditional, 401(k) match — all covered. **Implication: the near-term win is packaging/marketing what exists, not authoring head-term content.**
2. **The Tier-1 gap list is validated almost exactly.** Job-change/401(k) ("what happens to my 401k when I quit" is the #1 benefits cluster; 41.4% cash out), scams (young adults are the *most-scammed* cohort by frequency — 44% of 20-29 fraud reports lost money; $470M text-scam losses), salary negotiation, payday/cash-advance/overdraft, credit-report rights — every planned lesson has demand evidence behind it.
3. **Demand shape is shifting procedural.** Google reports "How do I…" queries at an all-time high (+25% YoY). Script-shaped content wins ("how do I get this fee waived" — Ramit Sethi's word-for-word script page ranks top-10). → Decision items should include *scripts*, not just verdicts.
4. **Every SERP winner has a conversion conflict.** Affordability queries are won by lenders/listing sites, PMI-removal by refi shops, gap insurance by dealers, BNPL questions by BNPL providers. The honest-educator position is genuinely unoccupied → reinforces the no-affiliate positioning as a stated differentiator.
5. **The seasonal calendar got richer.** Beyond open enrollment (Oct–Nov) and tax season: a **December FSA "use it or lose it" panic** (~$4.5B forfeited/yr), a **mid-February refund-shock window** ("why is my refund so low" — news-cycle scale in 2026), the **November IRS limits announcement**, and 2026's one-in-eleven-years **27-payday year** for biweekly earners.

## 3. Validated gaps — confirms the Block-1b plan

1. **Scams & fraud patterns** (×2 lessons) — fake bank/fraud-alert texts ($470M text-scam losses, FTC), Zelle/Venmo "can I get it back," gift-card scams ($217M+), job-offer/task scams (fresh FTC alert Apr 2026). Young adults are the most-scammed cohort by report frequency. Urgent-help demand shape; pattern-recognition decision items.
2. **Job-change money** (×2 lessons, upgraded from 1) — (a) "Your 401(k) when you leave": rollover options, the 20%-withholding indirect-rollover trap, cash-out math (41.4% cash out; ~85–90% drain fully — UBC Sauder/HBR); (b) "Health coverage when you leave": COBRA vs marketplace (marketplace typically far cheaper post-subsidy), the 60-day clock, vesting-timing cross-link. The #1 benefits cluster by volume and harm.
3. **Salary negotiation math** — validated (Harvard PON-dominated SERP; no calculator winner = open lane). Script-shaped items.
4. **Payday / cash-advance apps / overdraft** — validated; listicle-saturated SERP ("apps like Dave/EarnIn") signals urgent volume. Include the fee-waiver *script* (script-shaped demand).
5. **Credit-report rights** — free weekly reports, disputes, freezes (spiky with breach news). Fact-based, zero math.
6. **Tipflation decision items** — 9 in 10 say tipping culture out of control (WalletHub 2025); adds the judgment layer to existing tip math.

## 4. New discoveries — additions/changes to the plan

1. **Negative equity / "upside down on car loan"** (NEW lesson candidate) — Edmunds Q4-2025 records: 29.3% of trade-ins underwater, avg $7,214, 40.7% on 84-month terms; mainstream news pickup. We teach trade-ins but not negative equity ("the dealer pays off your loan" misconception = it's rolled in). Slots into Car module + Car Buying pack. ⚠ verify stats before authoring.
2. **BNPL lesson needs a currency fix, not just a stat refresh** — the credit-reporting reality *flipped* in Aug 2025: Affirm reports to bureaus; Klarna/Afterpay declined (Axios). Most 2024-era content is now wrong — including possibly ours. Elevate the `debt_bnpl_hidden_cost` refresh into Block 1b. ⚠ verify current reporting status per provider.
3. **Shrinkflation/unit price elevated to wave 1** — GAO (July 2025): unit-price increases of 12–32% from downsizing; 75% of Americans have noticed (Empower). Tiny lesson, native mental math, Smart Shopping module.
4. **Scripts as a content flavor** — procedural "how do I…" demand (+25% YoY) means decision-item explanations should carry the actual words to say (overdraft waiver, OTD-price email, negotiation counter).
5. **Web programmatic backlog (post-launch):** paycheck-calculator/[state] (the highest-volume calculator cluster: "paycheck calculator" ~50–136K, "take home pay calculator" ~146K, "tax calculator" ~316K per Semrush data on SmartAsset); doc-fee/[state] (CarEdge proves the pattern); PTO-payout/[state]. Plus seasonal pages: "why is my refund low" (February), "FSA deadline" (December).
6. **Moments calendar additions:** December FSA panic · mid-Feb refund shock · November IRS limits day · 27-payday-year January angle.
