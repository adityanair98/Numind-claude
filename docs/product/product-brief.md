# Product Brief

> **Last updated:** May 2026 (after V1 refinement — new modules, scoring reweight, streak freezes).

## Product
Numind is an iOS-first learning app that helps users build practical number sense through short daily lessons and quizzes. Calculators are deferred to the future website and are not part of the iOS app.

## Core Promise
Get faster at everyday money math in 5 minutes a day.

## Target User
Adults and young adults (roughly 22-40) who want to become faster and more confident with everyday math without feeling like they are using a school tutoring app.

They may feel slow or rusty with splitting bills, calculating tips, judging discounts, estimating totals, interpreting percentages, understanding loan costs, comparing salary offers, evaluating rent affordability, or understanding their tax bracket.

## V1 Wedge
Practical number sense across 10 topic areas:
- **Tips** - tip calculation, pre/post-tax, bill splitting
- **Discounts** - percentage off, BOGO, stacked discounts, unit pricing
- **Budgets & Spending** - budgeting, subscriptions, cost per use, sales tax, cart estimation
- **Salary** - net pay, raises, overtime, pay period conversion
- **Investing** - simple interest, Rule of 72, ROI
- **Estimation** - rounding, percent change, doubling/halving, multiply by 5
- **Debt** - credit card interest, minimum payments, loans, DTI, extra payments
- **Travel** - currency conversion, exchange fees, travel budgets, accommodation, insurance
- **Housing & Rent** - rent affordability (28% rule), rent increases, move-in costs, price per sqft, mortgage vs rent
- **Taxes & Paycheck** - marginal tax brackets, paycheck deductions, gross-to-net, tax refund estimation, self-employment tax

**Total: 10 modules, 47 lessons, 237 quiz templates**

## Core Loop
Home -> pick a lesson from the zigzag learning path -> read short lesson (concept + shortcut + example) -> quiz (5 questions first time, 8 on subsequent attempts) -> instant feedback per question -> session summary with XP, mastery star, personal best, badges -> progress update (streak, levels)

## Long-Term Monetization
Long onboarding -> diagnostic test -> preliminary evaluation -> personalized plan -> hard paywall with 7-day free trial.

## Immediate Build Priority
Core product loop first:
Home -> Learn (zigzag path) -> Lesson -> Quiz -> Feedback -> Summary -> Progress.

## Non-Goals For First Build
- no real payments
- no real backend
- no AI generation
- no social features
- no content CMS
- no Android
- no calculator tools (deferred to website)
- no push notifications (local reminders only)
