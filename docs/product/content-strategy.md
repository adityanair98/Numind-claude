# Content Strategy — Financial Math Topics & Audience Research

> **Last updated:** May 2026. Research-backed analysis of highest-demand financial math topics, audience hooks, and module prioritization.

## Market Context

- **Only 27%** of U.S. adults pass a basic financial literacy quiz (FINRA). Only 4% get all 7 questions right.
- **71%** confused by a compound interest question.
- **88%** of Americans say high school did not prepare them for handling money.
- **$388 billion** lost to financial illiteracy in 2023 alone.
- **Zero progress** in 8 years — 49% correct on the TIAA P-Fin Index in both 2017 and 2025.
- **Gender gap**: Women scored 45% vs men at 53%, but women outperform men when they do invest. The gap is confidence, not capability.
- **Confidence-competence gap**: Only 19% can explain common financial terms, yet 70% feel positive about their knowledge.
- **Market size**: Financial literacy app market is $4.1B (2025) growing at 13.9% CAGR.

## Target Audiences

### Audience A — Men (25-40): "Level Up"
Self-improvement culture. Want to feel sharp, make smart money moves, not get ripped off. Drawn to tactical, strategic framing. Active on self-improvement Reddit, finance YouTube, "hustle" culture.

**Language**: tactical, strategic, "the math behind...", "most people get this wrong", "the numbers don't lie", "level up your money game"

### Audience B — Women (22-38): "Build Confidence"
Want to stop feeling anxious about numbers, feel empowered making money decisions. Not intimidated by math itself — intimidated by financial jargon and the perception that "this isn't for me."

**Language**: "finally understand...", "stop feeling lost about...", "take control of...", "it's simpler than you think", "you deserve to know..."

**Key insight (Fidelity 2025)**: Women's top financial resolutions are save more (46%), pay down debt (41%), spend less (33%). 61% feel optimistic they'll be better off financially. The appetite is there — the numerical confidence to act is what's missing.

## Competitive Gap

Every existing financial literacy app teaches **concepts** (what is compound interest?). None teach the **math skill** (how to estimate compound interest in your head). This is the app's core differentiator in a $4B+ market.

## Topic Demand Tiers

### Tier 1 — Massive Search Volume (100K+ monthly searches)

#### Paycheck & Income Math
- "How to write a check" — 187K/mo (still #1 most-searched financial question)
- Gross vs net pay, reading a paycheck stub, understanding deductions
- Salary raise percentage calculations (is my 3% raise actually a pay cut after inflation?)
- Paycheck breakdown TikToks regularly get 1M+ views

#### Mortgage & Housing Math
- "Mortgage calculator" — 2.3M monthly searches
- "How much house can I afford" — extremely high volume
- Rent vs buy math, the 28/36 rule, closing costs (2-5%), down payment math (3% min, 20% for no PMI)
- 90% of 2024 first-time buyers had regrets (Bankrate)

#### Credit & Debt Math
- Credit score mechanics — top 5 most-searched financial topic
- APR to real dollars (24% APR = $1.97/day on $3,000 balance)
- Compound interest on credit card debt (daily compounding)
- Minimum payment trap math
- Credit card companies make $120B/year from consumer confusion about compounding

#### Student Loan Math
- #1 most-searched financial topic by raw volume (2.4M searches)
- Income-driven repayment plan calculations
- PSLF forgiveness (120 qualifying payments = 10 years)
- Refinancing math

### Tier 2 — High Volume (10K-100K monthly searches)

#### Tax Bracket Math
- Marginal vs effective rate — #1 most persistent tax misconception
- "Will a raise put me in a higher bracket?" — most harmful tax myth
- Self-employment tax surprise (15.3% on top of income tax)
- Quarterly estimated taxes for freelancers

#### Investing & Retirement Math
- "How to invest in stocks" — 32K/mo
- 401(k) compound growth, Rule of 72, Roth vs Traditional vs 401(k)
- Employer match = "free money" math
- Dollar cost averaging, index fund historical returns (8-10% annualized)

#### Budgeting Math
- 50/30/20 rule — referenced by every major bank
- "How to save money" — 31K/mo
- Emergency fund calculation (3-6-9 month rule)
- Subscription audit (average American spends $219/mo but estimates $86/mo)

### Tier 3 — Medium Volume, High Engagement

#### Insurance Math
- Deductible vs premium vs copay vs coinsurance vs out-of-pocket max
- High-deductible vs low-deductible plan comparison
- HSA triple tax advantage
- Annual spike during open enrollment season
- Widely cited as least-understood financial topic; zero app competition

#### Car Buying Math
- Monthly payment vs total cost (the dealer trick)
- How extending loan term lowers payments but increases total cost
- The 20/4/10 rule (20% down, 4-year max, 10% of gross income)
- High emotional stakes, dealer manipulation angle

#### Inflation & Purchasing Power
- Real return = nominal return minus inflation
- 3% raise with 4% inflation = actual pay cut
- $50,000 lifestyle today requires $121,000 in 30 years at 3% inflation
- 40% of Americans fail basic inflation question on FINRA quiz

#### Net Worth & FIRE Math
- Net worth calculation (assets minus liabilities)
- FIRE number (25x annual expenses), 4% safe withdrawal rate
- Savings rate to years-to-retirement mapping
- Strong overlap with self-improvement audience

#### Side Hustle / 1099 Tax Math
- Self-employment tax (15.3%) blindsides gig workers
- Quarterly filing requirements ($1,000+ threshold)
- Gig economy booming — millions getting 1099 income for first time

## Underserved Niches (Gaps in Market)

1. **The "math translation" gap** — Every app teaches concepts, none teach the skill of doing the math. This is the core differentiator.
2. **Insurance math** — #1 adulting pain point, zero app competition, seasonal demand spike.
3. **Side hustle tax math** — Gig economy boom, millions blindsided, highly hookable.
4. **"Is this a good deal?" snap estimation** — Pure number sense for daily decisions, daily utility.
5. **Car buying math** — High emotional stakes, dealer manipulation angle, very hookable.
6. **The women + math anxiety intersection** — Research directly connects math anxiety → financial anxiety → worse outcomes. Women outperform when they invest. The gap is confidence.
7. **FinTok fact-check** — 70% of viral finance TikToks contain misleading info. "The math behind the viral claim" positioning.
8. **Subscription/recurring cost awareness** — $133/month gap between perceived and actual spending.
