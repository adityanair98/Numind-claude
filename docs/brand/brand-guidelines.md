# Numind Brand Guidelines

## What Numind Is
Numind is a **financial education app that heavily uses math as its teaching method**. Brand decisions should reinforce that positioning — visual, copy, and motion language should feel like trusted, practical financial coaching, not a math drill or a brain-training app.

The differentiator from other financial-literacy products: we teach the *math behind* every concept (compound interest, marginal brackets, IDR, OOP max, etc.) so users can actually *use* the knowledge in real moments — at the restaurant, the dealership, during open enrollment.

## Brand Feel
Friendly, clean, modern, useful, lightly playful, financially credible.

## Logo
Primary logo is the brain-abacus app badge.
Wordmark is Numind with rounded friendly typography and teal dot accent.

## Colors
- Primary Blue
- Deep Navy
- Mind Teal
- Insight Yellow
- Soft Background
- Card Background
- Muted Gray

## Motifs
- beads
- rails
- brain-abacus (the brand mark: financial wisdom + mental calculation, fused)
- progress paths
- yellow insight bead

## Avoid
- school worksheet UI
- finance dashboard styling
- generic brain-training visuals
- childish mascot-heavy UI
- harsh red/green finance colors
- "math drill" framing — math is the teaching method, the product is financial literacy
- jargon-heavy "finance bro" copy — Numind talks to a 22-40 adult who's not a finance pro
