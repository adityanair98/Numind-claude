# Numind Brand Guidelines

## What Numind Is
Numind is a **financial education app that heavily uses math as its teaching method**. Brand decisions should reinforce that positioning — visual, copy, and motion language should feel like trusted, practical financial coaching, not a math drill or a brain-training app.

The differentiator from other financial-literacy products: we teach the *math behind* every concept (compound interest, marginal brackets, IDR, OOP max, etc.) so users can actually *use* the knowledge in real moments — at the restaurant, the dealership, during open enrollment.

## The Positioning Sentence (Phase 12)

> **Numind teaches the real-life money skills school never taught you — so when the decision shows up, you're ready.**

Three load-bearing parts: the **school gap** is the no-blame origin story ("you're not bad with money; nobody taught you"), the **moments** are the stakes, and **readiness** is the receipt. All user-facing promise copy should be traceable to this sentence.

## Language Rule: math is the method, never the promise

Our audience overlaps heavily with math-anxious adults — every "math" in promise copy reads as homework to them and as a drill app to everyone else. Mental math is HOW we teach (internal identity, CLAUDE.md); what the user buys is readiness.

- **Budget: at most ONE disarm per surface/funnel** — e.g. "You don't need to be a finance person. This isn't a test — it's a preview of what walking in ready feels like." (lives on the diagnostic intro). Drop the old "every shortcut is 20 seconds" speed claim everywhere.
- Everywhere else, sell the gain in the user's words: *"walk in ready," "know your number," "know the move," "what it's actually worth," "spot the trap," "keep the money you've earned," "money instincts."*
- "Math" / speed is welcome INSIDE the product where it lands as delight, not dread: shortcut reveals, explanations, teach cards, the Sharpness pill — the demo, not the pitch.
- Honest-progress vocabulary stays capability-based: skills mastered, skills to refresh, packs Ready — never points, levels, or grades.

## Positioning line: "Walk in ready."

The customer-facing positioning line (screen 1, paywall, App Store) is **"Walk in ready."** It beats speed/number-coded lines ("know your number") because it's true for 100% of the content — numerical *and* conceptual/decision — and it *is* the product's own progress model (`ReadinessEngine`). **Sell readiness, not speed.** The product teaches two kinds of readiness — numerical (estimate the number) and decision ("know the move") — and both ladder to one promise: *you know what to do when the decision shows up.* "20 seconds" describes only the numerical half; it is a retention delight (the Sharpness pill), never an acquisition claim. (Strategy: `docs/product/onboarding-repositioning-2026-06.md`.)

## Brand Feel
Friendly, clean, modern, useful, lightly playful, financially credible — and
**alive**. (The Phase 13 research reframed "premium" so it never reads as flat
or sterile: life comes from illustration, depth, motion, and earned celebration,
not from rainbow color, mascots, or per-answer confetti.)

> **Visual execution is governed by `design-language-spec.md`** (Phase 13,
> "Warm-Premium Money Tool"). This file owns voice, positioning, the language
> rule, motifs, and the honest-progress vocabulary; the spec owns the color/type/
> shape/illustration/feedback/motion system. They agree by construction.

## Logo
Primary logo is the Numind app badge, built on the beads-on-rails motif — beads
advancing along a rail (steady progress + mental calculation), without leaning on
a literal abacus or math iconography.
Wordmark is Numind with rounded friendly typography and teal dot accent.

## Mascot + glyph usage
Each module has a finance-coded mascot emoji + accent glyph (e.g. 🛒/%, 💰/$, 🏠/$).
Reserve the mascot+glyph pairing for **celebration moments only** (pack/world
completion, milestone sheets). On everyday surfaces (Worlds hero, Progress rows,
recent activity) prefer the module SF Symbol in the module accent color — the
mascot reads as childish when overused.

## Avoid
- school worksheet UI
- finance dashboard styling
- generic brain-training visuals
- childish mascot-heavy UI
- harsh red/green finance colors
- "math drill" framing — math is the teaching method, the product is financial literacy
- jargon-heavy "finance bro" copy — Numind talks to a 22-40 adult who's not a finance pro
